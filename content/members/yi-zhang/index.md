Yi Zhang has been a research associate at the Chair of Mechatronics since 2021. Yi holds a master's
degree in electrical engineering and information technology from the University of Stuttgart.

Yi's research develops probabilistic deep learning methods, in particular normalizing-flow-based Bayesian inference, for system identification, state estimation and data-driven control of physical systems under uncertainty, and applies them with industry partners in automotive, robotics and sensor manufacturing.
