Andreas Hofmann has been a research associate at the Chair of Mechatronics since 2022. Before that, he
worked on modeling and simulation at Bosch Rexroth and Robert Bosch GmbH.

His research combines physical modeling and machine learning for the simulation of mechatronic
systems. He coordinates the digital twin topic in the AI Production Network Augsburg.
