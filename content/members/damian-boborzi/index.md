Damian Boborzi has been a research associate at the Chair of Mechatronics since 2021. From 2018 to 2021
he was a doctoral candidate at ETAS GmbH in the Bosch doctoral program, supervised by the chair.

His research covers the generation of virtual traffic participants from traffic measurement data
with machine learning, and the use of normalizing flows in reinforcement learning and imitation learning.
