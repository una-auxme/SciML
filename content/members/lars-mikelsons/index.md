Lars Mikelsons has held the Chair of Mechatronics at the University of Augsburg since 2018.
Before that, he worked at Bosch Rexroth as a development engineer and team leader (2011–2016) and at
Bosch as a project manager (2016–2018).

His research focuses on reliable methods for model-based development, the validation of system
concepts with virtual prototypes, and the integration of model-based methods into modern
development processes.
