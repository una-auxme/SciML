With today's simulation methods and tools, the washing process in a car wash cannot be modeled
comprehensively. Improvements to car wash systems and wash programs are therefore developed and
validated in real tests with up to a hundred different vehicles, which takes weeks of effort.

**NACSIM** (*Neural Accelerated Carwash Simulation*) aims to model the entire washing process and to
simulate it with high performance, so that resource-, cost- and time-intensive real tests can be
replaced by simulations and thus reduced. The basis for this is the combination of physical
relationships and models with machine learning methods.

The project ran from June 2023 to July 2026 together with the industrial partner WashTec.
