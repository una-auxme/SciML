**UPSIM** (*Unleash Potentials in Simulation*) was a European research project in the ITEA 3 program
of the Eureka network, running from October 2020 to September 2023. Its 31 partners from industry
and academia in Austria, Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands, Romania and the United Kingdom worked on
making simulation a credible part of product development. Simulation already supports early design
decisions, virtual testing and predictive maintenance, but product quality and certification still
rely on real tests. UPSIM enabled companies to collaborate on simulations safely, reproducibly and
reliably, and to use them as *credible digital twins*.

Our group contributed hybrid modeling with **NeuralFMUs**: simulation models exported as Functional
Mock-up Units (FMUs) are combined with neural networks, which learn the effects that the physical
model does not capture. NeuralFMUs are available in the open-source Julia packages
[FMI.jl](https://github.com/JuliaFMI/FMI.jl) and [FMIFlux.jl](https://github.com/JuliaFMI/FMIFlux.jl).
The publications below were funded in part by UPSIM.
