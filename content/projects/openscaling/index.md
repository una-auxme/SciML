**OpenSCALING** (*Open standards for SCALable virtual engineerING and operation*) is a European
research project in the ITEA 4 programme of the Eureka network, running from November 2023 to
January 2027. Its 26 partners from industry and academia in France, Germany, Hungary and Sweden
enhance open standards for modeling and simulation, such as Modelica, FMI and SSP, and the tools
built on them, so that they support large-scale systems and distributed controllers. The aim is to
optimize such systems for low energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

Industrial demonstrators apply the results in the energy, building, aviation and automotive domains,
for example in green hydrogen production, more efficient heat pumps, fuel cell propulsion and
electrified vehicles.

Our group contributes to the project's work on **physics-enhanced neural ODEs (PeN-ODEs)**: hybrid
models that extend physical simulation models with neural networks, and their integration into
Modelica-based workflows.
