We are always looking for motivated people who enjoy working at the boundary between mathematics,
engineering and machine learning.

## Open positions

Open positions in our group, for example for doctoral researchers and student assistants, are
announced on the website of the Chair of Mechatronics:

<p><a class="btn btn-primary" href="https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/fakultaet/fai/informatik/prof/imech/open-positions/">Open positions at the Chair of Mechatronics</a></p>

No suitable position at the moment? You are still welcome to get in touch with us.

## Theses

We supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses in computer science, mathematics and engineering.
If one of our [projects](/projects/) sounds interesting, write to us with a short CV and your
transcript of records.

## How to apply

For advertised positions, please follow the instructions in the job posting. For theses and all
other inquiries, send your documents as a single PDF to
[sciml@informatik.uni-augsburg.de](mailto:sciml@informatik.uni-augsburg.de).
