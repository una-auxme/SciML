Researchers from different disciplines revisited the priority research directions for Scientific
Machine Learning set out in 2018 and discussed the future research agenda of the field in a keynote,
technical talks and breakout sessions. The workshop was hosted by the Centre for Advanced Analytics and
Predictive Sciences (CAAPS) at the University of Augsburg.
