[Yi Zhang](/members/yi-zhang/), [Lars Mikelsons](/members/lars-mikelsons/) and co-authors published *[A comparative study of convolutional neural networks and vision transformers for quantitative phase analysis from X-ray diffraction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rineng.2026.113313)* in *Results in Engineering*.

We train a single multi-task model that simultaneously classifies phase presence and regresses phase fractions on mixed-phase XRD patterns, and compare a 1D-CNN against a ViT under identical conditions using a large simulated dataset spanning waste-relevant crystalline phases, together with a small set of real laboratory measurements.
