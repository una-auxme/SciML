At [JuliaCon 2026](https://juliacon.org/2026/) in Mainz (10–15 August), our group gave three talks and
presented two posters in the poster session:

- [Julian Trommer](/members/julian-trommer/) presented *[Optuna.jl – Hyperparameter optimization with Optuna in Julia](https://pretalx.com/juliacon-2026/talk/A9PDA7/)*,
  which brings the widely used optimization framework Optuna to Julia.
- [Valentin Höpfner](/members/valentin-hoepfner/) showed in *[Differentiating Functional Mock-up Units (FMUs) with Enzyme](https://pretalx.com/juliacon-2026/talk/KKHD78/)*
  how LLVM bitcode embedded in FMU binaries enables fast automatic differentiation of black-box simulation models.
- [Tobias Thummerer](/members/tobias-thummerer/) gave an overview of what is possible with FMUs in Julia today in
  *[Big simulation models suddenly feel very small – with FMI.jl](https://pretalx.com/juliacon-2026/talk/F8L7TA/)*.

Many thanks to Julian, Valentin and [Josef Jouaux](/members/josef-jouaux/) for their contributions. The talks were
livestreamed and can be watched on the [Julia YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TheJuliaLanguage/streams).
