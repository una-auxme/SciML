[Tobias Thummerer](/members/tobias-thummerer/) received the *Wissenschaftspreis der Universitätsstiftung
Augsburg* (science prize of the University of Augsburg Foundation) for his doctoral thesis
*[Scientific machine learning for hybrid modeling in real world engineering: Validated on examples from automotive and medical industry](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/130046)*.
It is the second award for the thesis this autumn, after the
[dissertation award of the Centre for Interdisciplinary Health Research (ZIG)](/news/2026-10-dissertation-award-tobias/)
in October. Congratulations!
