[Tobias Thummerer](/members/tobias-thummerer/) and [Lars Mikelsons](/members/lars-mikelsons/) published *[Eigen-informed neural ordinary differential equations: Incorporating knowledge of system properties](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2025.131358)* in *Neurocomputing*.

It is shown that eigen-informed neural ODEs are able to converge in fewer steps, generalize better and are solvable more efficiently compared to pure neural ODEs, and can even reconstruct a given system on undersampled data.
