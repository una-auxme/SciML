On 10 June 2026, [Tobias Thummerer](/members/tobias-thummerer/) took part in the kick-off of the symposium
*Lost in Time 2026 – Temporal Patterns & Time Series in Medicine* at the IHK Schwaben in Augsburg.
Clinicians, data scientists, students and industry partners came together to discuss time-dependent
medical data, from physiological monitoring to wearable sensors and patient trajectories, and to make
new connections. Thanks to Kora Helm, Ennio Idrobo-Ávila, Sebastian Zaunseder and Fabian Fahlbusch for
organizing the event, hosted by the Department of Neonatology and Pediatric Intensive Care at the
University Hospital Augsburg and the Chair of Diagnostic Sensing at the University of Augsburg
([flyer, PDF](https://www.uk-augsburg.de/fileadmin/Daten/Kliniken/Kinderklinik_Augsburg_-_MKZS/Kinder_und_Jugendliche_II/Dateien/Flyer/Lost-in-time-english.pdf)).
