[Yi Zhang](/members/yi-zhang/), [Lars Mikelsons](/members/lars-mikelsons/) and co-authors released the preprint *[Deep Spectral-Sequential Networks: Learning Phase Transition Dynamics from Raman Spectra](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7182382)* on SSRN.

In this study, we introduce a deep-learning-based method to predict a material's phase diagram from a series of correlated Raman spectra. To identify the optimal model, we compared four different architectures, each pairing a spectral encoder with a sequence regressor.
