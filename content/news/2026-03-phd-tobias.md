On 16 March 2026, [Tobias Thummerer](/members/tobias-thummerer/) successfully defended the doctoral thesis
*[Scientific machine learning for hybrid modeling in real world engineering: Validated on examples from automotive and medical industry](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/130046)*
at the Faculty of Applied Computer Science of the University of Augsburg, supervised by
[Lars Mikelsons](/members/lars-mikelsons/). The thesis is available open access
([PDF](https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/files/130046/Thummerer_Diss.pdf)).

Tobias now leads the newly established [Scientific Machine Learning research group](/) at the Chair of Mechatronics.
