[Andreas Hofmann](/members/andreas-hofmann/), [Lars Mikelsons](/members/lars-mikelsons/) and co-authors published *[Diode\_jl: New Ways for simulation analysis and Scientific Machine Learning of Dymola models in Julia](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jocs.2026.103038)* in *Journal of Computational Science*.

This paper presents Diode_jl, an open-source software tool that translates the intermediate representation of Dymola simulation models into well-structured Julia source code, enabling advanced numerical diagnostics and Scientific Machine Learning workflows for Modelica-based models.
